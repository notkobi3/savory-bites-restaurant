About Savory Bites

Founded in 2020, Savory Bites began as a small family-owned restaurant with a big dream: to create a dining experience that brings people together through exceptional food and warm hospitality.

Our head chef, Maria Rodriguez, brings over 20 years of international culinary experience, having trained in Italy, France, and Japan before returning to create our unique fusion menu.

We believe in sustainable practices, supporting local farmers, and creating a workplace where our team can thrive and grow.
